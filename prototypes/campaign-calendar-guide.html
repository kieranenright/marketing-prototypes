Campaign Calendar: Intake Guide

How to submit your campaigns and initiatives for the shared marketing calendar.

1 What You Need to Provide

Each submission has two parts: the initiative (the campaign as a whole) and the entries (each individual touchpoint within it). You fill in one spreadsheet and share it with Kieran for import.

One row = one touchpoint. A 3 day email drip with one creative brief = 1 row with a start and end date. Three separate emails with different content = 3 rows.

You can submit one initiative or your full quarter in a single sheet. Group entries by initiative.

2 Initiative Level Information

This is the "parent" information that applies to all entries underneath it. Fill these in on the first row of each initiative.

FieldRequiredDescriptionExample
Initiative Name Required The name of your campaign or initiative Summer Campaign, Rewards Launch, Win-back Q3
Point of Contact Required Who owns this initiative overall Your name
Objective Required One sentence describing what this initiative aims to achieve "Drive reactivation and trial through seasonal summer messaging"
Product Release Optional If this campaign supports a product release, specify which one. This links your campaign to the Product view so stakeholders can see coverage. See release list below
Markets Required Which markets this initiative applies to (comma separated) US, UK, DE, AU, CA
Jira Epic Optional The Jira epic or ticket ID for the creative request covering this initiative. All entries below will inherit this link. CREA-456 or full Jira URL
Airtable Link Optional Airtable view or record URL for CRM tracking. Inherited by all entries below. Full Airtable URL

3 Entry Level Information (Per Touchpoint)

Each row after the initiative header represents one campaign touchpoint. Fill in the following for each.

FieldRequiredDescriptionExample
Entry Text Required Short description of this specific touchpoint "Launch email sequence", "Meta prospecting broad", "IG teaser reel"
Channel Required Primary channel category (see list below) CRM, Paid, Organic
Subchannel Required Specific channel type (see list below) Email, Push, Meta, IG
Owner Required Who is responsible for this entry Marina K, Domi, Isabela
Start Date Required When this entry goes live (YYYY-MM-DD format) 2026-06-02
End Date Optional When this entry ends. Leave blank for single day sends. 2026-06-15
Status Required Current status (see definitions below) Planned, In Progress, Live, Completed

How inheritance works: Fill columns A through D (Initiative Name, POC, Objective, Release) on the first row of each initiative. On subsequent rows for the same initiative, you can leave them blank or repeat them. The Jira Link on the first row becomes the epic link for the whole initiative. Individual entry rows can override with their own ticket.

8 How to Submit

Option A: Google Sheet (recommended)

  1. Open a new Google Sheet
  2. Use the column headers from the template above as your first row
  3. Fill in your initiative and entries
  4. Share the sheet with Kieran

Option B: CSV Upload

  1. Fill in the CSV template file
  2. Open the Campaign Calendar
  3. Click the upload icon (top right) to import your CSV directly

Option C: Give it to Claude

  1. Fill in the spreadsheet or CSV
  2. Upload the file to Claude with the prompt: "Please import this into the campaign calendar"
  3. Claude will validate the data and update the calendar JSON

Common Questions

How do I log a multi day campaign (e.g. a paid flight running June 2 to June 15)?
Use the Start Date and End Date columns. One row covers the entire flight.
How do I log a 3 email drip with one creative brief?
One row, with Start Date as day 1 and End Date as day 3. The single Jira ticket covers all three.
What if I have 3 separate emails with different content?
Three rows, each with its own date, text description, and (optionally) its own Jira ticket.
My campaign doesn't relate to a product release. What do I put?
Leave the Release Link column blank. Your initiative will still appear in the Product Marketing, CRM, Leadership, and Markets views.
What if my release isn't listed?
Include the release name, launch date, description, and markets in your submission. Kieran will add it to the system.
Can I update my entries after submitting?
Yes. If you have editor access, you can click any entry in the calendar to edit it directly. Otherwise, share an updated sheet with Kieran.
How do I get editor access?
Ask Kieran. You'll log in with your email on the calendar page.
What views will my data appear in?
All views: Product Marketing (year/month/week), Product (if linked to a release), CRM (if channel is CRM), Leadership (all initiatives), and Markets (filtered by country).
Do I need to keep statuses updated?
Yes, please. The CRM and Leadership views rely on accurate status data to show what is active, what is coming, and what has completed.
