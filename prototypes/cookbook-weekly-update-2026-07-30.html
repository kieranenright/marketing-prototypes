Peak Campaign — Cookbook Promotion
W36 + W37 Preview
Cookbook will feature in the Peak campaign emails (W36 + W37) with dedicated app promotion modules. CTAs are deeplinked to Cookbook, allowing us to attribute Cookbook events (views, saves) back to the Peak campaign.
Deeplinked Attributable
Why this matters: First time Cookbook has a dedicated presence in a major campaign email. Deeplinks will let us measure the full funnel from email open through to recipe save, giving us a clear read on how existing subscribers respond to Cookbook promotion at scale.
