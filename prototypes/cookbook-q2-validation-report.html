Organic Social
What we learned.
Five validated insights from 10 weeks of testing across formats, channels, and markets.
Learning 01
"That's me" memes drive the highest organic reach
Content tapping personal frustration ("lost another recipe", "hunting through reels") consistently hits 8K+ views. Observational humour caps at 4.4K. The hook must make the viewer the main character.
9.9K best 4.4K worst
Learning 02
Produced creative-led video generates 3x the engagement of UGC demos
Jorge Orozco's produced video hit 2.3% engagement vs 0.77% average for UGC product demos. Higher production value and personality drive comments. Generic feature walkthroughs do not generate conversation.
2.3% produced 0.77% UGC demo
Learning 03
UGC product demos have a hard organic ceiling of ~2.5K
Regardless of concept (C3, C4, C5, C6, C9, C12a), 9:16 UGC product walkthroughs consistently land 1.3K to 3.7K views. Not gaining algorithmic traction. Needs paid amplification to perform.
~2.2K avg reach Ceiling confirmed
Learning 04
Carousels are the only format generating profile follows
Best engagement rate (0.92%) and the only format to produce profile visits and followers. Ideal for education/storytelling content that drives deeper consideration.
0.92% eng +Follows
Learning 05
Problem framing outperforms feature education. Always.
Content that leads with a relatable problem ("never lose another dinner idea", "hunting through a reel") consistently outperforms content explaining how Cookbook works. Audiences engage with the pain point before they care about the solution. This holds across all formats and all markets tested.

CRM / Lifecycle
Push notification
test results.
Two trigger-based push notification tests ran in Q2. One clear win, one clear signal to avoid.

Post-Pausing Push

Validated
Sending Cookbook content to customers who paused their deliveries. Test period: Apr 16 – 30. 90% confidence interval.
MetricMean LiftCI (90%)Significant?
Cancellations -6.60% [-11.2%, -1.8%] Yes
Engagement +1.97% — No
Adoption +1.34% — No
Recommendation: Roll out to all paused customers, once per month. Statistically significant reduction in cancellations with no negative side effects.

Post-Meal Selection Push

Do Not Scale
Nudging active subscribers toward Cookbook after they select meals. Test ran in May, 90% CI.
MetricMean LiftCI (90%)Significant?
Pause Rate +2.02% — Yes
Finding: Pushing Cookbook content to active subscribers at meal selection increases pausing. Hypothesis: it signals "you can cook without us" at the wrong moment. This trigger should not be scaled.
Playbook Recommendations
What to do next.
Based on 10 weeks of validated data, here is the recommended approach for Q3 and beyond.
Organic Social
  • 1.Lead with "that's me" memes for reach (2-3x/week)
  • 2.Invest in produced creative-led video for engagement (1x/week)
  • 3.Test more carousels for profile growth
  • 4.Only boost UGC product demos with paid
  • 5.Always lead with problem, not feature
Conversion Path (Critical Fix)
  • 1.Fix LiB landing page to surface Cookbook
  • 2.Add deep link from social directly to Cookbook
  • 3.Re-measure after fix (expect >5% click-to-view)
  • 4.Creator UTM links must bypass LiB entirely
CRM / ASO
  • 1.Scale post-pausing push to all markets
  • 2.Kill post-meal selection push
  • 3.Iterate CPP screenshots (7% CVR too low)
  • 4.Submit Apple featuring with updated creatives
