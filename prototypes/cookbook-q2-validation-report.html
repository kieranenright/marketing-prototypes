CRM
One win, one kill.
Two trigger-based push notification tests. One statistically significant reduction in churn. One to avoid entirely.
Scale this

Post-Pausing Push

-6.6%
cancellation rate (statistically significant, 90% CI)
Cookbook push to paused customers reduces churn with no negative side effects. Roll out to all markets, once per month.
Kill this

Post-Meal Selection Push

+2.0%
pause rate increase (statistically significant)
Pushing Cookbook after meal selection signals "you can cook without us." Increases pausing at the worst possible moment. Do not scale.
