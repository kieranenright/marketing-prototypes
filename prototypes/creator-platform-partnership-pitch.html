Addressing Concerns

Pre-empting the two most common questions

Concern

"It adds complexity to the brief"

It's one additional line item. The creator links their videos (which they're already producing for the partnership). HelloFresh handles the entire conversion to recipe cards. There's no additional creative direction, no review rounds, no assets to collect. The brief addition is literally: "Create a HelloFresh Creator Profile and link your partnership recipe videos."

Concern

"Creators won't want to do it"

This isn't extra unpaid work. It's an additional benefit we're offering them. They get: global distribution to millions of users, founding creator status (first 150 only, never offered again), product roadmap influence, and a permanent profile on a platform used in 16+ countries. Most creators would pay for this kind of reach. We're giving it to them as part of the partnership.

💡

Reframe: This isn't something we're asking creators to do for us. It's something we're offering them. The pitch is: "As part of this partnership, we'll also set you up with a Creator Profile on our app so your recipes reach millions of additional users across 16 countries."

Where This Goes Next

The Vision

Menu Integration
