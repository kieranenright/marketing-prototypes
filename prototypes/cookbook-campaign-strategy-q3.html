Pillar 2 · Paid Social

Make them aware
Cookbook exists.

Feature awareness, not acquisition. Paid social in Q3 is laser focused on surfacing Cookbook to existing HelloFresh app users and lapsed subscribers who don't know the feature exists. HF-produced brand creative, not creator content. Aspirational lifestyle positioning that shows the value of saving and cooking recipes beyond the box.

Inspire % saving external/creator recipes

Awareness is the prerequisite to save behaviour.

Users who don't know Cookbook exists can't save to it. Paid social unlocks the top of the save funnel by showing existing users what's possible beyond the meal kit.

Act Planning Adoption & Discover Save Rate

Creative positions the planner as the hero action.

Paid ads land users in Cookbook via deep link. Creative narrative shows the full loop: save, plan, cook. Every new user who enters via paid is a potential planner adopter and Discover browser.

Paid Social Business Case

The Challenge

Cookbook awareness is critically low and external recipe saves remain flat.

64%

of surveyed customers did not know you can save an external recipe

5.6%

of all recipes saved are external. The metric is flat.

The audience most likely to use this feature discovers recipes in their social feeds, and we have no way to reach them there at scale. Organic social only reaches existing active followers. CRM only reaches active and lapsed subscribers. Paid social is the only channel that unlocks prospects: people who save recipe videos every day but have never used HelloFresh.

Without paid investment, Cookbook remains invisible to the users it was built for: people scrolling TikTok and Instagram, saving recipe videos they will never cook because they have no system to act on them.

Why Now

Organic validation is complete. 10 weeks, 25+ posts, 4 markets, 7/8 internal KPIs passed. The question is no longer "does this resonate?" but "how fast can we scale?"

Back to school timing (Aug 1 to Sep 15). Peak meal planning intent: families resetting routines, students cooking independently for the first time. Aligns directly with Cookbook's core use case.

The awareness gap is widening. Every week without paid amplification is a week where prospects continue saving recipe videos into a void, unaware that Cookbook exists to solve exactly that problem.

Inspire % saving external/creator recipes

Creator recipes ARE the metric.

Every creator recipe saved counts directly toward the North Star. More creators means more content worth saving, which lifts the percentage of active users engaging with external/creator recipes.

Act Planning Adoption & Discover Save Rate

Fresh, recognisable creator recipes in Discover drive higher session-level save rates. Users who save creator recipes are more likely to assign them to the planner, lifting planning adoption.
