The gap we're closing

What happens when people save recipes from elsewhere

People who only save HelloFresh recipes, compared with people who've also saved at least one recipe from elsewhere. That one outside save changes everything: they stick around about 3× longer.

Segment Users % of total Avg saves W1 retention W8 retention
Only save HelloFresh recipes 387K 94.4% 4.4 7.8% 3.4%
Also save recipes from elsewhere 23K 5.6% 9.5 (2.2× more) 18.2% (2.3×) 10.3%

Creator traffic: 0 direct recipe saves or app downloads attributable to creator profile visits to date. The funnel infrastructure is being built now to make this convert.

Q3 north star · The pivot

% of active users saving external or creator recipes.

We were measuring bookmarks, not behavior. People who also save recipes from elsewhere don't just save more. They stay 3× longer. The shift from absolute savers to % saving external/creator targets the engaged core, gives creator content an audience to land in, and ties growth to real activation.

Pillar 2 · Paid Social

Make them aware Cookbook exists.

Feature awareness, not acquisition. Paid social in Q3 is laser focused on surfacing Cookbook to existing HelloFresh app users and lapsed subscribers who don't know the feature exists.

↗ North star link

Can't save external recipes if you don't know Cookbook exists. Paid social reaches users that push can't: those who haven't engaged yet.

How do we surface Cookbook to dormant app users?

Foundation

Controls, measurement, and quality.

The shared infrastructure that makes the three pillars work together without cannibalising each other.

Suppression

5-day global frequency cap

No user receives more than one Cookbook push in any 5-day window, across all use cases.

Deep-linking

Every push lands on content

All pushes deep-link to the specific recipe, collection, or Cookbook section referenced in the copy.

Personalisation

Dynamic recipe + timing

Recipe names, cook times, saved counts, and protein types are injected per-user from Cookbook data.
