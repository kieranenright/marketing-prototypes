The Shift

Q2 proved the concept.
Q3 narrows the focus.

Q2 was broad: social content, ASO, paid, CRM, creators, all running in parallel to validate what works. Q3 takes the highest impact levers and goes deep.

Q2: Broad Validation

  • Social first: organic content across TikTok, IG, FB
  • ASO: custom product pages + featuring nominations
  • CRM triggers introduced (post-meal, post-pause, post-download)
  • Creator partnerships: first collabs live

Q3: Focused Execution

  • Push notifications as the primary adoption and engagement lever
  • Paid social raising Cookbook awareness among existing and lapsed users
  • Creator recipes brought directly into the app at scale
Q3 North Star · The Pivot

% of active users saving external or creator recipes.

We were measuring bookmarks, not behavior. People who also save recipes from elsewhere don't just save more. They stay 3× longer. The shift from absolute savers to % saving external/creator targets the engaged core, gives creator content an audience to land in, and ties growth to real activation.

The Q3 Framework

Three pillars, one foundation.

Each pillar answers a different question on the path to the north star.

P1: Push Notifications

Do users engage with Cookbook?

16 use cases, 39 touchpoints across 4 phases. Behavioural triggers that surface Cookbook at pivotal moments.

P2: Paid Social

Do users know Cookbook exists?

Feature awareness for existing app users. HF-produced brand creative on Meta and TikTok.

P3: Influencer Marketing

Is there content worth saving?

Automated creator sourcing + internal team partnerships. Recipes from creators land in the app at scale.

Foundation: Recipe quality + HelloFresh structured in the URL/share advantage. AI + structured data for all recipes enables scale across all stages.

Pillar 2 · Paid Social

Make them aware
Cookbook exists.

Feature awareness, not acquisition. Paid social in Q3 is laser focused on surfacing Cookbook to existing HelloFresh app users and lapsed subscribers who don't know the feature exists. HF-produced brand creative. Aspirational lifestyle positioning.

How do we surface Cookbook to dormant app users?

Push handles the engaged. Paid reaches the rest.

Why Paid Social in Q3

Q2 proved Cookbook works organically. But discovery remains the bottleneck: most existing app users still haven't opened the Cookbook tab. Push notifications handle engaged users. Paid social reaches people scrolling Instagram and TikTok who already have HelloFresh installed but haven't discovered the feature.

How It Connects

Paid social creates awareness. Push notifications convert aware users into active ones. Creator recipes give them content to save once they arrive. The three pillars form a funnel: See it (paid) → Use it (push) → Love it (creator content).

Creative approach: HF-produced brand creative, not creator content. Aspirational lifestyle positioning that shows the value of saving and cooking recipes beyond the box. Feature awareness goal, not direct acquisition.

Pillar 3 · Influencer Marketing

Two approaches.
One creator ecosystem.

Q3 builds a scalable influencer engine for Cookbook. From sourcing creators via automation to monetising their recipes inside the app, each approach feeds into the next.

Where does the content come from?

Bring supply of external recipes and the creators who make them. Without external content, the KPI theme stalls.

External Tools: Automated Creator Sourcing

Use external tools to scrape Instagram and automatically identify high-potential food creators for Cookbook partnerships. Replaces manual outreach with a scalable discovery pipeline.

External Tools → Scrape IG → Identify → Qualify → Outreach

Internal Team Partnerships

Leverage existing influencer relationships across HelloFresh teams to fast-track Cookbook creator onboarding, and turn loyal subscribers into micro-influencers who promote Cookbook to their own audiences.

Influencer Team: Re-engage for Cookbook-specific briefs
Loyalty Members: Equip active members as advocates

How they connect: Source (External Tools + Internal Teams) → Partner (Onboard to Cookbook) → Create (Recipes in the app) → Scale (Loyalty members amplify). The two approaches feed the same pipeline from different directions.
