Send Logic
Scheduled send. Not event-triggered. Max 1 per 7 days. Suppressed if event-triggered push sent in last 5 days.

PM window • Max 1/7 days • Deep link: Cookbook home
Global Rules
Frequency Cap
Max 1 Cookbook push per 5 days across all use cases
AM Window
07:30 – 09:00 local. Planning mindset, morning scroll.
PM Window
17:00 – 19:00 local. Dinner decision moment.
24h Suppression
No push if user received any other push in past 24h
Deep Link Standard
Link to specific recipe when personalised; Cookbook home when generic
Priority Order
Removed Meal > Gap Fill > Planned Night > Missing Protein > High-Rated > Skip > Delivery > Pause > Trending
Batch Coverage Override
Batch cook detected (portions >2 or 2+ recipes same day) suppresses gap-fill for covered days
Personalisation Fallback
If recipe name >20 chars in title, move to body with generic title. Never truncate mid-word.
Opt-out Respect
Immediate suppression on push opt-out. No fallback to other channels from this strategy.

Pillar 2: Paid Social
Make them aware
Cookbook exists.
Feature awareness, not acquisition. Paid social in Q3 is laser focused on surfacing Cookbook to existing HelloFresh app users and lapsed subscribers who don't know the feature exists. HF-produced brand creative, not creator content. Aspirational lifestyle positioning that shows the value of saving and cooking recipes beyond the box.
2
Platforms
Feature
Awareness Goal
Why Paid Social in Q3
Q2 proved Cookbook works organically. But discovery remains the bottleneck: most existing app users still haven't opened the Cookbook tab. Push notifications handle engaged users. Paid social reaches the rest: people scrolling Instagram and TikTok who already have HelloFresh installed but haven't discovered the feature.
How It Connects
Paid social creates awareness. Push notifications convert aware users into active ones. Creator recipes give them content to save once they arrive. The three pillars form a funnel: See it (paid) → Use it (push) → Love it (creator content).
Platforms
Meta Ads
Instagram Feed, Stories, and Reels. Highest overlap with existing HelloFresh app users. Precise custom audience targeting via app activity signals.

TikTok Ads
In-Feed and TopView placements. Recipe content is native to the platform. Vertical video format matches Cookbook's social DNA perfectly.

04 Monetise Recipes & Audiences
Revenue Models
Give creators a reason to stay. Two monetisation paths that turn their recipes and audiences into income.
One-Time Purchase (OTP)
Creator audiences can buy a single meal kit without subscribing. Lowers the barrier for their followers to try HelloFresh.
Recipe Slots (incl. HF Ingredients)
Creator recipes get dedicated slots in the meal kit, with HelloFresh supplying the ingredients. Revenue share back to the creator on every purchase.
💰
Revenue Share Model
Creators earn on every slot purchased. Aligns incentives: more engagement = more income for both sides.
