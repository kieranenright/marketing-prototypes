Why Feature This
Why this feature deserves featuring
Genuine user value
Solves a universal pain point: turning scattered social media videos into structured, cookable recipes. Users go from inspiration to action in seconds, not minutes of rewinding and pausing.
Broad audience, zero barrier
Completely free, no subscription required. This opens HelloFresh to a much wider audience: anyone who watches cooking content on social media and wants to actually cook it.
Strong retention signal
The feature creates a personal, growing recipe library. Every saved recipe is a reason to come back. Users build habits around saving, browsing, and cooking from their collection.
Built for
everyday use.
It meets users where they already are. Millions of people save recipe videos every day across TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook. The Cookbook connects directly to that behaviour through the native share sheet, creating a seamless bridge between discovery and cooking.

It grows with the user. Every recipe saved makes the app more personal and more valuable. The new community features (profiles, following, collections) mean users come back not just to cook, but to discover what others are making. This is the kind of engagement loop that drives long-term retention.
Accessibility
We are committed to making the Cookbook inclusive and accessible to all users. Improvements are on our roadmap to ensure the feature works for every home cook regardless of ability.
