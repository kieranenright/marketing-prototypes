CookUnity Chef Outreach — Feedback Review
US Market
Reviewed all responses from the 24 CookUnity chefs contacted. Clear, repeated topics have emerged across the feedback, giving us a strong signal on what any partnership proposal needs to address upfront.
Recurring Themes
ThemeSignalImplication
IP / Ownership Repeatedly raised Creators want clarity on who owns the content once it lives on Cookbook
