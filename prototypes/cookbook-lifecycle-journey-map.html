Weekly Lifecycle: Push Touchpoints & Cookbook Opportunities

Mapping every push notification and activation opportunity across the HelloFresh weekly cycle. From meal selection through cooking and beyond. Where does Cookbook fit, and where are the gaps?

New Opportunity
📋

Selection Window (Menu Live → Cutoff)

The user is browsing the weekly menu, adding and removing meals. Decisions are still in flux. This is the "consideration" phase where intent signals fire.

Day 1 of Cycle
Menu Goes Live
New weekly menu is available. User can browse, add meals to their box, and explore options. Cookbook recipes are also visible alongside kit meals.
Opportunity
Cookbook Recipe Pairing
"Picking your box? Your Cookbook has sides and extras that pair with this week's menu." Surface saved recipes that complement what's on the menu this week.
AM
During Selection
User Adds & Removes Meals
User interacts with the menu. They add meals to their box, swap them, and remove options they're unsure about. Removing a meal and saving it to Cookbook fires a key event.
Phase 2
Removed Meal Reminder
User adds meal → removes it → saves to Cookbook. Push fires same day PM or next morning. "That {{recipe_name}} though. Already in your Cookbook."
PM / Next AM
Opportunity
Save Before You Lose It
If user removes a meal but does NOT save it to Cookbook, nudge them to save it. "Dropped {{recipe_name}}? Save it to Cookbook so it's there when you want it."
Same day PM
Cutoff (e.g. Sat 8pm)
Meals Locked In
Choices are final. The user knows exactly which 3 or 4 meals are coming. This is the transition from "choosing" to "planning the week." Momentum is high, intent is clear.
Phase 1
Plan Your Week
Fires morning after cutoff. "Your meals. Your nights. Your call." / "30 seconds now saves decisions all week." Drives planner adoption.
Next AM 07:30
Opportunity
Fill Your Off Nights (Pre-delivery)
User has 3 meals locked in but 7 nights ahead. Immediately post-cutoff: "3 nights sorted. What about the other 4? Your Cookbook has ideas." Targets the planning mindset while it's active.
Next AM
📦

Box Delivery

The box arrives. Ingredients are in the fridge. The user transitions from planning to execution. This is the highest-engagement moment of the weekly cycle.

Delivery Day
Box Arrives
Delivery confirmation triggers. Ingredients are physically in the home. This is the peak activation moment: freshness is high, excitement is real, dinner is imminent.
Phase 0
Box Day Cookbook Activation
First-time Cookbook users only. "Cooked tonight? It's already in your Cookbook. Save it and mark it cooked to keep it forever." Establishes the save + cook habit.
PM 17:00
Phase 2
Box Delivery Day (The Other Nights)
For active Cookbook users. "Box is here. What about the rest? 3 nights sorted. Your Cookbook handles the gaps." Fires day after delivery.
Next AM 07:30
Phase 2
Missing Protein Suggestion
"No chicken this week? Your {{recipe_name}} needs it. One trip, one dinner sorted." Surfaces saved recipes that use proteins not in this week's box.
Next AM 07:30
Opportunity
Cookbook Discovery at Unboxing
The moment they open the box, they're already thinking about food. In-app interstitial (not push): "While you unpack: browse 30-min Cookbook recipes for the nights your box doesn't cover."
In-app
🍳

Cooking Week (Delivery → Next Cutoff)

The user cooks their way through the week. Some nights use the box, some nights are open. This is where the planner, Cookbook, and habit loops all converge.

Kit Nights
Planned Meal Kit Dinner
User has a meal kit recipe assigned in their planner for tonight. Ingredients are in the fridge. The plan exists, this push reinforces it.
Phase 1
Cook Tonight
"Tonight: {{recipe_name}}. You planned it. Ingredients are in. Tap to start cooking." / "{{cook_time}} mins. That's it." Reinforces the plan, drives cook-through.
PM 17:00
Opportunity
Post-Cook Cookbook Cross-Sell
After they finish cooking (mark as cooked or 2h after recipe open): "Liked that? Save a similar one to Cookbook for next time you want {{cuisine_type}}." Bridges kit to Cookbook discovery.
PM 20:00+
After Cooking
User Rates Meal (4-5 stars)
User just cooked and loved it. They rate it highly. The emotional peak: satisfaction, accomplishment, "I want to make this again." Cookbook ownership moment.
Phase 2
High-Rated Meal Kit
"{{recipe_name}} is a keeper. Save it. Cook it again. No kit needed." Fires 2 hours after a 4 or 5 star rating. Drives recipe ownership in Cookbook.
+2h after rating
Opportunity
Creator Recipe Match
"Loved {{recipe_name}}? A creator in Cookbook has a version with a twist." Connect high-rated meals to similar creator recipes. Drives Cookbook depth and creator platform engagement.
Next AM
Off Nights
No Kit Meal Assigned
Days with no meal kit in the planner. The "what's for dinner?" moment. This is Cookbook's primary real estate: they're already in planning mode, just without a kit answer.
Phase 2
Meal Planner Gap Fill
"Tonight's wide open. {{recipe_name}} from your Cookbook. {{cook_time}} mins, your call." / "No kit tonight. No problem. Your Cookbook. Your rules."
AM 07:30
Phase 2
Planned Night Reinforcement
If user has assigned a Cookbook recipe to tonight's slot: "Tonight's sorted. {{recipe_name}} is on your planner. Tap when you're ready to cook."
AM 07:30
Opportunity
Creator Spotlight (Off Night)
"No box tonight? @{{creator_handle}} just posted a 20-min recipe. Save it and cook tonight." Use off-nights to introduce creator content when user has no plan yet.
PM 16:00
Batch Day (e.g. Sunday)
Batch Cook / Meal Prep
User assigns 2+ recipes to one day or a high-portion recipe. They're batch cooking to cover multiple days. The system must recognise this and suppress false gap triggers.
Phase 2
Batch Cooking Detection
"Meal prep mode: on. {{x}} dinners handled in one session. We won't bug you till {{next_day}}." Acknowledges the batch, suppresses gap triggers for covered days.
PM 17:00
Opportunity
Batch-Friendly Recipe Suggestions
Surface Cookbook recipes tagged as "meal prep friendly" or "makes great leftovers" when we detect batch behaviour. Help them fill more of the week in one session.
AM (batch day)
Any Day
General Re-engagement
Scheduled sends not tied to a specific event. For users who haven't been activated by an event trigger recently. Low-frequency, broad targeting.
Phase 3
General Cookbook (Forgotten Saves / Low Effort / Try New)
"Remember what you saved?" / "No plan? No problem. Open your Cookbook." / "Cook something different. Your Cookbook has recipes you haven't tried yet."
PM 17:00
Phase 2
Trending Recipe
"Everyone's making this right now. {{trending_recipe}}. {{save_count}} saves and counting." Social proof trigger when a recipe hits threshold saves on the platform.
PM (when threshold hit)
Opportunity
Seasonal / Creator Collection Drop
"New this week: 5 summer grilling recipes from @{{creator}}." Use creator content drops as a push trigger. Low frequency (1x/month max), high novelty.
AM
⚡

Edge Cases (Skip / Pause / Cancel)

When the box doesn't arrive. These are retention-critical moments where Cookbook provides continuity and keeps the user in the HelloFresh ecosystem without a subscription.

Skip Week
No Box This Week
User skipped their delivery. They still need to eat. The app would normally go silent. Cookbook fills the void with free value and keeps the user active.
Phase 2
Skip Week
"No box. No problem. Your Cookbook's got you covered. {{saved_count}} recipes ready to go." Fires day 1 of skip week. Continuity, not winback.
AM 07:30
Opportunity
Skip Week Cookbook Challenge
"No box this week. Try 3 Cookbook recipes instead?" Gamify the skip week by framing it as a Cookbook experiment. Mid-week check in: "2 down, 1 to go."
Day 1 AM
Pause / Cancel
Subscription Ends
User pauses or cancels. The highest-risk churn moment. Cookbook is free and doesn't require a subscription. Keep the app installed, keep the habit alive.
Phase 2
Post-Pause / Cancel
"Paused the box. Not the cooking. Your Cookbook is always free. Save from anywhere, cook on your terms." Fires 48h after event. Retention through utility.
+48h AM
Opportunity
Creator Follow Prompt (Post-Cancel)
"Still cooking? Follow creators in Cookbook for new recipes every week." Position creator content as the ongoing value hook that keeps them in the app after cancellation.
+7d

New Cookbook Activation Opportunities (Not Yet in Push Strategy)

Selection Window

Cookbook Recipe Pairing

Surface saved Cookbook recipes that pair with this week's box meals (sides, desserts, breakfast). Positions Cookbook as a meal companion, not a separate feature.

Selection Window

Save Before You Lose It

Nudge users who remove a meal but don't save it to Cookbook. Capture intent before it disappears. "Dropped it? Save it for later."

Post-Cutoff

Fill Your Off Nights (Pre-delivery)

The moment meals lock in, the user knows they have 3 or 4 covered nights. Prompt Cookbook planning for the remaining nights while planning mindset is active.

Delivery Day

Cookbook Discovery at Unboxing

In-app interstitial (not push) during or after unboxing. "While you unpack: browse 30-min Cookbook recipes for your other nights." Catches peak food enthusiasm.

Post-Cook

Creator Recipe Match

After a high rating: connect the loved meal to a similar creator recipe in Cookbook. "Loved this? A creator has a version with a twist." Drives Cookbook depth and creator content.

Off Nights

Creator Spotlight

On unplanned off-nights, surface a trending creator recipe as the answer. "No box tonight? @creator just posted a 20-min recipe." Introduces creator platform value.

Batch Day

Batch-Friendly Suggestions

When batch cooking is detected, suggest Cookbook recipes tagged as meal-prep-friendly or "great leftovers." Help them cover more of the week in one session.

Skip / Post-Cancel

Creator Follow + Collection Drops

Use creator content as the ongoing value hook post-cancellation. "Follow creators for new recipes every week." Monthly collection drops as a push trigger.
